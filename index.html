Graduate Research Assistant, ASER Lab
Department of Electronic Engineering, Kwangwoon University, Seoul

I fabricate polymer nanofibers and functional materials for energy harvesting and sensing. At ASER Lab I independently electrospin PVDF-HFP, PEO, and PA6, control fiber morphology through the process parameters, and build the mats into triboelectric devices. My training in textile engineering helps me take those materials onto fibers, yarns, and fabrics.
