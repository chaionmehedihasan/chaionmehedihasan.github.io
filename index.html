Research

My work sits between textile materials and electronic devices. The aim is not commodity fabric processing and not silicon process engineering. I design and process polymer composites and nanofibers, place them on soft or textile substrates, and measure them as triboelectric and wearable sensing layers.

Current focus

  • Polymer nanofiber and film fabrication for wearable devices
  • Triboelectric nanogenerators and self-powered sensors
  • Functional coatings and smart-textile substrates
  • Electrical output under controlled mechanical input

Background that I use

  • Fiber, yarn, and fabric structure from textile engineering
  • Multifunctional textile coatings (conductive, antibacterial, flame-retardant)
  • Polymer nanocomposite processing
  • Collaboration with physical-sensor and biosensor teammates in ASER Lab

Methods I use

Fabrication

Electrospinning, polymer film preparation, spin coating, thermal processing (oven / vacuum oven), electrode work including laser-induced graphene when needed.

Electrical measurement

Linear motor and force-controlled tapping, oscilloscope, electrometer, transferred charge and current, LCR.

Structure and surface

SEM, FTIR, thickness measurement, contact-angle measurement.

Reliability (building)

Wash, stretch, and humidity tests on wearable samples — added when the experiment is closed, not as a machine list.
